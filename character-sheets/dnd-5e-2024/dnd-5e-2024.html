Spells

ⓘ

Spells lists every spell you have, by level, with the box to prepare it and ● on the always-prepared ones. A Wizard has three lists: Prepared (what you can cast today), Known and the Spellbook, from which a Wizard prepares. Spells from a subclass, feat, species or item are always prepared (●) and do not count. Level up asks for the new cantrips and spells (a Wizard writes them in the Spellbook) and lets you replace one; subclass spells come by themselves at their level. A Bard, Sorcerer or Warlock changes class spells on level up, the other casters with Prepare spells. Add, remove and set the Origin of spells in the list at the bottom.
