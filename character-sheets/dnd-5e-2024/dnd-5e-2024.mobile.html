Active Effects ·

Nothing active. Set exhaustion, concentration and conditions below.

Exhaustion − to AC, spell save DC, saving throws, ability checks, attack rolls and damage; Speed −10 ft. Level 6 is death. A long rest removes 2 levels, Lesser Restoration 1, Greater Restoration all.

Exhaustion − to every d20 Test; Speed − ft. Level 6 is death. A long rest removes 1 level.

Concentrating . Taking damage: Con save, DC 10 or half the damage (up to 30).

Blinded Can't see; fail any check that needs sight. Attacks against you have Advantage, yours have Disadvantage.

Charmed Can't attack the charmer or target it with harmful abilities; it has Advantage on social checks against you.

Deafened Can't hear; fail any check that needs hearing.

Frightened Disadvantage on checks and attack rolls while the source is in sight; can't willingly move closer to it.

Grappled Speed 0. Disadvantage on attacks against anyone but the grappler, who can move you with it.

Incapacitated No actions, Bonus Actions or Reactions; can't speak; Concentration ends. Disadvantage on Initiative.

Invisible Advantage on Initiative. Attacks against you have Disadvantage, yours have Advantage, unless the other can see you.

Paralyzed Incapacitated, Speed 0. Fail Str and Dex saves. Attacks against you have Advantage; a hit from within 5 ft is a Critical Hit.

Petrified Incapacitated, Speed 0. Fail Str and Dex saves. Attacks against you have Advantage. Resistance to all damage; immune to Poisoned.

Poisoned Disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks.

Prone Crawl, or stand up for half your Speed. Disadvantage on your attacks; attacks against you have Advantage within 5 ft, Disadvantage beyond.

Restrained Speed 0. Disadvantage on Dex saves and on your attacks; attacks against you have Advantage.

Stunned Incapacitated. Fail Str and Dex saves. Attacks against you have Advantage.

Unconscious Incapacitated and Prone, drop what you hold, Speed 0. Fail Str and Dex saves. Attacks against you have Advantage; a hit from within 5 ft is a Critical Hit.

Saves, skills, initiative, AC and the attack bonuses on this sheet already include the exhaustion penalty.
